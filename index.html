Scope of Work

Done-for-you build, co-authored with owner

1) Monetize Time: Capacity Index + Priority Start

  • Weekly Capacity & Scheduling post template and cadence
  • Define and price Priority Start (limited rush slots per month)
  • Messaging scripts for Facebook/Messenger, SMS, and Google Business Profile (GBP)

2) Monetize Choice: Good–Better–Best Packages + Financing

  • Three clear roof packages per material type with warranties and upgrades
  • Financing integrated on every quote (0% promo and long-term options)
  • Proposal language, line-item alternatives, and price escalation clause addendum

3) Monetize Density: Corridor Dominance + Routing Rules

  • Service radius finalized (~25 miles around 44618) and two dominance corridors:
    • Dalton–Orrville–Wooster
    • Massillon–Navarre–Brewster
  • Routing rules to maximize revenue per mile and reduce windshield time
  • Minimum job size and referral-out policy for sub-minimum work

4) Monetize Proof: Photo Checklist + Review Engine

  • 20-point CompanyCam photo checklist embedded into your install flow
  • Remote measurement workflow (EagleView/Hover) to reduce site visits and rework
  • Review prompts that cue specifics (clean-up, communication, on-time), with SMS/email templates

5) Monetize Relationship: Roof Health Plan

  • Annual plan structure (inspection, gutter clean, minor tune-ups, priority storm response)
  • Enrollment flow (QR/Stripe), member leave-behind card, renewal messaging
  • Optional pre-storm drone baselines for faster, cleaner insurance claims

6) Strategic Assets and Dashboard

  • The McDonough Method: estimating templates, install SOPs, customer scripts, warranty process
  • Alpha Dashboard: average ticket, premium-tier selection rate, Priority Start uptake, close rate, profit per crew-day, cycle time, review rate, callback rate, revenue per mile
  • 10-minute weekly readout and adjustment cadence

Appendix C: 20-Point Photo Checklist (Summary)

  • Arrival/Protection (yard, landscaping, neighbor notice)
  • Decking exposure and repairs
  • Underlayment, ice-and-water at eaves/valleys
  • Starter, drip edge, valley metal
  • Flashing (chimney, walls, skylights), pipe boots
  • Ventilation (ridge/box/solar), baffles where applicable
  • Penetrations sealed and documented
  • Ridge/hip cap installation
  • Perimeter/cleanup: magnet sweep, debris haul-out
  • Final overview shots (front/back/left/right), homeowner walkthrough

Appendix D: Typical Investment Ranges (Dalton area)

  • Asphalt shingle replacement: $9,500–$18,500
  • Standing-seam metal: $18,000–$38,000
  • Flat/low-slope (EPDM/TPO/mod): $10,000–$24,000
  • Repairs/tune-ups: $250–$1,800

Variables: pitch, layers, valleys/dormers, decking repair, designer shingles, custom flashing.
